Art style: Rick and Morty cartoon style - a modern adult-animated sitcom look: clean black outlines of even medium weight with a slightly wobbly hand-drawn feel, flat cel colours with ONE hard-edged darker shade tone per colour (no airbrush gradients, no painterly texture), slightly lopsided goofy proportions, simple shapes, crisp and readable. Faces (where there are any) have big round white eyes with tiny dot pupils, wobbly eyelids and a loose grimacing or grinning mouth.
Lighting: a warm gas-lamp key light from the upper left plus a faint magical green rim glow on the lower right; the shade tone always sits on the lower-right side of every form.
Palette: New Orleans voodoo night on Bourbon Street - Mardi Gras purple #5B2A86, royal violet #7E3FB0, Mardi Gras gold #F2B230, brass #C9952E, Mardi Gras green #2E9E4F, spooky voodoo green glow #7CFF6B, deep bayou teal #1F4E5A, night navy #1A1F3D, blood red #B3263A, bone #EDE3C8, cream #F3E6CF, black #141414.
